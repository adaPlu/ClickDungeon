# ClickDungeon Store Copy

## Short Description
ClickDungeon is a fast, tile-based dungeon crawler built for short mobile runs.

## Full Description
ClickDungeon is an offline-first dungeon crawler focused on quick runs, tactical
tile reveals, and persistent RPG progression.

Explore multi-floor dungeons, uncover traps and treasure, battle monsters,
manage your gold, and build your hero across repeated sessions. Each class has
its own progression path and combat style, with runs designed to feel good on
touch devices without demanding long play sessions.

Highlights:
- Tile-based dungeon exploration with traps, keys, treasure, and boss floors
- Four playable classes: Knight, Ranger, Thief, and Wizard
- Combat with telegraphed enemy actions and animated battle panels
- Shop, buyback economy, achievements, and offline encrypted save slots
- Accessibility-focused options including color-blind mode

ClickDungeon is intended for players who want a compact dungeon RPG that works
well in short sessions while still offering meaningful progression and replay
value.
