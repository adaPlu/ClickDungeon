## Summary

ClickDungeon is an offline-first mobile game. The game stores save data locally
on your device and does not require account creation or login of any kind.
No personally identifiable information (PII) is collected or transmitted.

---

## Data We Store

- Local save data for game progress
- Local settings such as audio, difficulty, and accessibility preferences
- Local inventory, achievements, and progression metadata

All data listed above is stored exclusively on your device and is never
transmitted to our servers or any third party by this app directly.

---

## Crash and Analytics Data

The release build of ClickDungeon uses:

- **Firebase Crashlytics** (provided by Google LLC) to collect anonymous crash
  reports. Reports contain device model, OS version, app version, and a stack
  trace. No personally identifiable information is included.
- **Firebase Analytics** (provided by Google LLC) to collect anonymous usage
  events such as screen views and in-game actions. No personally identifiable
  information is collected.

Crash reporting and analytics are disabled in debug builds.

Data collected by Firebase is processed by Google LLC under their Privacy
Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## How Data Is Used

- To persist game progress between sessions
- To store gameplay settings selected by the player
- To maintain inventory, achievements, and progression state
- To diagnose crashes and improve app stability (via Crashlytics)
- To understand aggregate usage patterns and improve the game (via Analytics)

---

## Data We Do Not Collect

- No real name, email address, phone number, or other personal identifiers
- No location data
- No payment or financial information (in-app purchases are not active in v1.0)
- No data is sold to third parties under any circumstances
- No account or login is required to play

---

## Security

ClickDungeon stores save data locally and protects sensitive preference data
with encryption and integrity checks where supported by the device platform.

## Children

ClickDungeon does not knowingly collect personal information from children
under the age of 13. The app does not require account creation or any form
of personal data entry.
